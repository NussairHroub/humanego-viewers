Pipeline

How it works

  1. Frames: every 2nd frame of demo 000 — 460 frames total, deliberately including the walk-in approach, which is what gives the reconstruction its room-wide coverage (the manipulation window alone only sees the table).
  2. Lift: MoGe-2 depth per frame, lifted to 3D uncropped up to 6 m, with the arm masked out where a mask exists and each point carrying its pixel's RGB. Per-frame metric scale is set by the median ratio against the MPS semidense SLAM points (median scale 0.832).
  3. Fusion: all ~11.4 M points pool into a 1.5 cm voxel grid — per-voxel mean position and mean color — and only voxels seen from ≥ 9 distinct frames survive, which removes single-frame depth phantoms. Result: 211,306 colored voxels.
  4. Display: a mild luma percentile stretch and small chroma gain are applied at bake time so the room reads well on screen (viewing, not radiometry).
  5. Assembly: the fused cloud replaces the semidense cloud in the standard pose viewer — the 64 registered demo trajectories and the red RealSense test camera are identical to the original page.

Honest caveats

  • MoGe depth on the textureless black tabletop has low-amplitude (cm-scale) warp.
  • Coverage follows attention: areas the glasses only glimpsed for a few frames are culled by the view filter, so the room extent is slightly tighter than the sparse cloud's.

Built with a standalone reconstruction script (MoGe-2 lift + vectorized voxel-mean fusion with distinct-view counting) and make_web_viewer.py --cloud, which renders per-point colors in both the WebGL and Canvas-2D paths. Same npz schema as the true-color semidense cloud.
