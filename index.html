HumanEgo — 3D viewers

Interactive 3D views of egocentric Aria demos: registered camera trajectories, room point clouds and reconstructions, and the point-cloud observations fed to the manipulation policy. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, shift-drag to pan. Each construction has a page explaining how it was generated.
