HumanEgo — 3D camera-pose viewers

Interactive 3D views of egocentric Aria demos: the fused semidense room point cloud plus each demo's registered camera frustums and head trajectory. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, shift-drag to pan, and use the slider to step through demos.
