V3 fused scene reconstruction

Instead of lifting a fresh (noisy) scene cloud every frame, all of a recording's V2 clouds are fused into one static reconstruction of the workspace — the same scene points are shown to the policy at every timestep.

open (height-shaded) → open (true color) →

Cross-view voxel fusion

How it works

  1. Take every frame's V2 dense scene points (already metric-scaled, arm-masked, in the shared world frame).
  2. Pool them into 1 cm voxels; each voxel's position is the median of its points.
  3. Keep only voxels supported by at least max(10, 3% of frames) distinct frames — transient depth phantoms don't accumulate support and vanish.
  4. The result is one scene_recon.npz per recording. Training samples it once (FPS to 1024 points) and shows the same scene at every timestep alongside the per-frame V1 entity points.
  5. At robot inference the symmetric move: the scene is reconstructed once from the RealSense after homing, then reused for the whole episode — zero per-step segmentation or depth lifting.

The two variants

  • Height-shaded: scene colored by height above the fitted table plane — good for judging geometry (flatness, phantoms, object relief).
  • True color: a finer fusion (5 mm voxels, ≥5 views) with per-point median RGB sampled from the frames — ~44k colored points; good for judging what the reconstruction actually is.

Built with preprocess/PointCloudGen.py --recon (fusion via utils/pcd.py::fuse_voxel_median, unit-tested on synthetic phantom data), colorization by projecting fused points into the recording's RGB frames, and the viewer pair extract_pcd_seq.py + make_pcd_viewer.py with a static-scene layer.
