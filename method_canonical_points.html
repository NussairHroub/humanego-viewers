Canonical points from SAM 3D

A prototype experiment: reconstruct each task object from ONE image with SAM 3D Objects, register the reconstruction into the demo world, and sample a fixed set of canonical points in the object's own frame — the same physical points in every demo, instead of per-recording keypoint picks.

Why

The V1 pipeline selects object keypoints per recording from a segmentation mask, so "the cup's 20 points" are different physical points in every demo. Point-policy methods get their power from correspondence — the same semantic points everywhere. A complete 3D reconstruction of the object gives that automatically: sample K points once in the object's local frame, and every demo (and robot deployment) re-poses the identical points via its own object pose.

Pipeline

  1. Reconstruct: the first pre-grasp frame's rgb.png + mask_objN.png go through SAM 3D Objects (single-image → complete textured mesh, including unseen surfaces; ~35 s per object on an A6000). Vertex colors are sampled from the mesh texture at the UVs.
  2. Register: trimmed similarity ICP (scale + SE(3), Umeyama updates) fits the mesh points to the recording's world — targets are the V1 triangulated keypoints (upweighted 8×) plus the fused scene reconstruction cropped around the object. Multi-start over yaw and flip handles the unknown output orientation.
  3. Sample: the registered points are mapped into the object's local frame via its latched pose T_obj_to_world, and farthest-point sampling keeps K=20 canonical points.

The recovered sizes are physically plausible and both objects sit correctly on the fused table in the viewer. The cup's keypoints hug the reconstruction tightly; several plate keypoints ring just outside the reconstructed disc — a flat disc leaves the in-plane registration weakly constrained, so the plate is the part to tighten (pin its vertical to the table plane, its scale to the rim keypoints) before training on canonical points.

Honest caveats

  • One frame of one recording so far; the value claim — the same canonical points across all 64 demos — still needs a cross-demo consistency check.
  • SAM 3D also predicts its own object pose per image (recorded, not yet verified against the SLAM-triangulated poses).
  • SAM 3D outputs are normalized-scale; metric scale here comes entirely from the ICP anchors.

Viewer features

  • Five toggleable layers: true-color reconstructions, canonical points, V1 triangulated keypoints, MPS hand landmarks, fused scene.
  • The frame-slider variant steps through all 566 manipulation-window frames — per-frame hand landmarks, V1 keypoints, grasp state, and the moving egocentric camera frustum (slider, play, ←/→ keys). The objects and canonical points stay put by the latched-anchor convention; the hand and camera are what move.
  • Drag = rotate, scroll = zoom, shift-drag or right-drag = pan; touch and pinch-zoom supported.
